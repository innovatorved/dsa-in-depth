Li Chao Segment Tree & Monotonic Convex Hull Trick DP Engine

A dynamically allocated Li Chao Segment Tree and a Monotonic Convex Hull Trick queue for optimizing O(n^2) dynamic programming recurrences of the form dp[i] = min_j (m_j * x_i + b_j) to O(n log X) and O(n).

##Algebraic Reduction of Quadratic DP to Lower Envelope of Lines

Consider the classic frog/partition DP recurrence: $$\text{dp}[i] = C + \min_{j < i} \left( (x_i - x_j)^2 + \text{dp}[j] \right)$$ Expanding $(x_i - x_j)^2 = x_i^2 - 2 x_j x_i + x_j^2$ separates the $i$-only and $j$-only terms from the bilinear cross term:

$$\text{dp}[i] = (x_i^2 + C) + \min_{j < i} \Big( \underbrace{(-2 x_j)}_{m_j} \cdot x_i + \underbrace{(x_j^2 + \text{dp}[j])}_{b_j} \Big)$$

Each state $j$ defines a line $y = m_j x + b_j$, and computing $\text{dp}[i]$ is simply evaluating the lower envelope of all previous lines at $x = x_i$!

Why Li Chao Trees Need Only One Recursive Call Per Level

Two non-parallel lines intersect at at most one point. Suppose at interval $[l, r]$ with midpoint $M$, line $L_{\text{win}}$ is lower than $L_{\text{lose}}$ at $x = M$. Then $L_{\text{lose}}$ can cross below $L_{\text{win}}$ on $[l, M]$ or on $[M+1, r]$, never both!

By storing $L_{\text{win}}$ at the current segment-tree node and pushing $L_{\text{lose}}$ into the single sub-interval where it crosses below $L_{\text{win}}$, every addLine and query visits at most $O(\log(\text{maxX} - \text{minX}))$ nodes—even when slopes and query points arrive in completely arbitrary order.

Guided Implementation Walkthrough (4 Progressive Steps)

Step 1: Midpoint Dominance Swap in Li Chao Tree

  • Core Mechanical Concept: Midpoint Dominance Swap in Li Chao Tree
  • Target Module: index.js (Step1)
  • Engineering Specification: At interval $[l, r]$ with midpoint $mid$, evaluate both newLine and node.line at $l$ and $mid$. If newLine is strictly smaller at $mid$, swap node.line and newLine so node.line is always the winner at $mid$.
  • Verification Invariant: verifies Step 1: Midpoint Dominance Swap in Li Chao Tree

Step 2: Single-Branch Intersection Recursion

  • Core Mechanical Concept: Single-Branch Intersection Recursion
  • Target Module: index.js (Step2)
  • Engineering Specification: Because two distinct lines intersect at most once, the losing line at $mid$ can beat node.line on at most one half of $[l, r]$ (the left half iff leftBetter !== midBetter). Recurse only into that half in $O(\log X)$ time.
  • Verification Invariant: verifies Step 2: Single-Branch Intersection Recursion

Step 3: Root-to-Leaf Envelope Evaluation

  • Core Mechanical Concept: Root-to-Leaf Envelope Evaluation
  • Target Module: index.js (Step3)
  • Engineering Specification: Implement query(x) by taking the minimum of node.line.eval(x) across all $O(\log X)$ nodes on the path covering $x$.
  • Verification Invariant: verifies Step 3: Root-to-Leaf Envelope Evaluation

Step 4: O(n) Monotonic Deque Convex Hull Trick

  • Core Mechanical Concept: O(n) Monotonic Deque Convex Hull Trick
  • Target Module: index.js (Step4)
  • Engineering Specification: When slopes $m_j$ decrease monotonically and queries $x_i$ increase monotonically, maintain the lower envelope in a deque using cross-multiplied intersection comparisons and a monotone pointer.
  • Verification Invariant: verifies Step 4: O(n) Monotonic Deque Convex Hull Trick
